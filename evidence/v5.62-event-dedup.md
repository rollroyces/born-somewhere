# v5.62 — Deduplicate life events

**Date:** 2026-10-22

## Problem observed

The life-events feed in v5.61 showed obvious repetition. A single life produced:

```
41  Armed men in the city — Take it and move
41  Armed men in the city — Take it and move
41  Diagnosed with cancer
40  Armed men in the city — Take it and move
40  Diagnosed with cancer
40  Your father is conscripted. Months of silence.
40  Your father is conscripted. Months of silence.
40  Armed men in the city — Take it and move
...
39  Armed men in the city — Take it and move
38  Armed men in the city — Take it and move
38  A job in another city — Take it and move
37  Armed men in the city — Take it and move
36  Had health issue
```

Three independent causes, all "draw from a pool with no memory":

1. **War events** — `pick([...5 items])` re-drawn every year with no record of what already fired.
2. **Illness** — `pick(['cancer','heart','stroke','diabetes'])` re-drawn even when `life.conditions[cond]`
   was already `true`, so "Diagnosed with cancer" could appear twice in one life.
3. **Yearly flavour events** — the age-banded pool in `generateYearlyEvent()` had no memory either, so
   "畢業了" / "有了孩子" / "結婚了" recurred three or four times each.

## Fix

**War** — each of the 5 war lines fires at most once per life; once all 5 are used, no more war events.

**Illness** — only conditions not already recorded in `life.conditions` are eligible.

**Flavour events** — added a `rep: true` flag marking the 11 that are genuinely repeatable daily
texture (fever, cold, new friend, family trip, vacation, checkup, grandchildren visit, park walk,
great book, health check, sick-for-a-week). Everything else fires at most once. When an age band's
one-shot events are exhausted and it has no repeatable ones, the year simply gets no flavour line
rather than repeating "Got married".

Both memories use plain arrays, not `Set` — the whole life object is serialised by the
`lifeExport` button, and a `Set` becomes `{}` on round-trip.

Two events were initially mis-classified as repeatable and corrected after measurement:
- `Affected by local unrest` — fired **11×** in Lebanon (its weight of 50 swamped the age pool).
  Made once-only, weight dropped 50 → 20.
- `Had health scare` / `Had health issue` — fired 13× / 5×. A health scare is notable, not routine.
  Made once-only.

## Verification — Lebanon (high conflict), full life, soft mode off

| Metric | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Log entries | 99 | 69 |
| Unique texts | 57 | 59 |
| Repeats that should not happen | 8 kinds | **0** |
| War lines | repeated 2–3× each | **5 lines, 1× each** |

Remaining repeats are all intentional daily texture:

| Text | Count |
|---|---|
| Health checkup | 5 |
| Took vacation | 5 |
| Got sick for a week | 2 |
| Had health check | 2 |

The earlier "Got married ×5 / Got divorced ×3 in one life" contradiction is gone — that was caused
by the fallback re-drawing from the exhausted full pool, which now returns `null` instead.
