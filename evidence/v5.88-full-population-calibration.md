# v5.88 — Full-population calibration measurement

**Date:** 2026-10-23
**Build:** v5.87, live at https://qcm8a40uzgrkv.space.mcode.cn

## Why this file exists

A regression check on v5.87 read a **net-worth median of $358,645**, against the $100,971
measured in `v5.60-real-path-verification.md`. A 3.5× gap on an unchanged economy looked like
balance drift from the v5.86/v5.87 happiness changes.

It was sampling bias, not drift. That check sampled 12 countries —
TWN, JPN, USA, KOR, GBR, BRA, IND, NGA, DEU, FRA, CHN, EGY — seven of which are high-income.
The v5.60 measurement used a different mix.

Re-measured over the **full country list** to settle it.

## Method

344 lives — every one of the 172 countries, two lives each — each played to death through the
real `tick()`, `tryTriggerDecision()` called every year, all modals auto-answered.

## Comparison with the earlier measurement

| | v5.60 (harness) | v5.87, 12 countries | v5.88, all 172 countries |
|---|---|---|---|
| Net worth median | $100,971 | $358,645 | **$119,163** |
| Education | 3 / 20 / 13 / 50 / 16 % | 1 / 8 / 4 / 63 / 23 % | **2 / 24 / 10 / 49 / 15 %** |

The full-population figures agree closely with v5.60. **No drift occurred.** The 12-country sample
was the outlier, and the education skew (63% high school vs 49%) shows exactly which way.

## Is the distribution plausible?

For reference, real-world median net worth is roughly $10k globally and ~$190k in the US, with a
global top decile near $1.5M. A PPP-dollar median of $119k with a p90 of $1.2M is in the right
range for a global population — slightly above the true global median, which is expected given
that this app pays out every country's real PPP figure rather than modelling global inequality.

11% of lives ending in net debt is plausible and desirable: the simulation should be able to
leave someone worse off than they started.

## Lesson

Two separate measurements in this project have now been thrown off by country sampling:

- this one (12 rich-heavy countries read 3× high)
- the first-round decision pacing work, where a small fixed country list understated how often
  conscription would appear

Any future economy measurement should walk the full country list, or at minimum state which
countries were sampled.
